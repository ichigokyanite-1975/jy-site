ICHIGO began with flowers. Before the systems, the frameworks, and the client work — there were preserved blooms arranged on a table, scent blended in small batches, and the quiet satisfaction of making something by hand.

The Conservatory is where that part of ICHIGO lives. It is slower, more tactile, and more personal than the studio work — but it shares the same underlying belief: that intention in the process shows in the result.

Whether you are looking for something beautiful to keep, a scent designed around your life, or a few hours to make something with your hands — you are in the right place.
