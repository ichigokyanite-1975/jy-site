Aromatherapy is one of the oldest forms of intentional care. Not a trend, not a shortcut — a practice rooted in the relationship between scent, the nervous system, and the rhythms of daily life.

At ICHIGO, we approach it with the same care we bring to everything else: with structure, with intention, and with genuine attention to the person in front of us. Every consultation begins with listening. Every blend begins with you.

Whether you are navigating stress, sleep, emotional balance, or simply looking to build a more considered daily ritual — we work with you to find what fits.

How a
consultation
works

A consultation is a conversation, not an assessment. There is no right or wrong answer — only what is true for you at this point in time. This is how we approach it.

01

We begin with listening

You share what is present for you — physically, emotionally, practically. There is no agenda. We are simply trying to understand what your body and life actually need right now, not what a standard protocol would suggest.

02

We explore the options together

Based on what we've discussed, we explore scent profiles, application methods, and timing. You are involved in every choice — the blend should feel right to you, not just correct on paper.

03

We formulate your blend

Your custom blend is prepared with care — proportions considered, intentions noted. You receive it with clear guidance on how to use it, when, and why each element is there.

04

We follow up

A good blend evolves with you. We check in after you have had time to use it, and adjust if needed. The intention is not a one-time transaction — it is a practice that grows.
