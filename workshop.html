Why we
run workshops

Most things we own are made elsewhere, by someone else, for everyone. A workshop is the opposite of that. It is a few hours set aside to make something with your own hands — something that carries the memory of the time you spent making it.

At ICHIGO, our workshops are small and unhurried. We guide you through the process, explain the materials and the choices, and give you the space to make it your own. You leave with something you made — and something you know.

We run sessions at partnered venues across Singapore, and are also available for private bookings — for teams, celebrations, or simply a group of friends.

01 花
Floral Workshop

Preserved
Flower Dome

Design and arrange your own preserved flower dome — a glass cloche filled with dried and preserved blooms that will last for years without water or care. Each dome is completely your own: you choose the flowers, the colours, and the arrangement.

Preserved flowers retain their shape and texture long after they are dried, making each dome a lasting piece you can display at home or give as a gift.

Aromatherapy Workshop

Scent &
Craft

A hands-on session where you work with essential oils and create your own aromatherapy product to take home. Sessions can focus on a single product or mix across formats depending on your group's interest.

Each format is guided with explanation — you learn what each oil does, how blending works, and why the ratios matter. You leave with both a product and an understanding of how to use it.
