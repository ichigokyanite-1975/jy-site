Booking
policy

We are a small independent studio. These terms exist so we can prepare properly and deliver the session well.

Advance Notice
All bookings require a minimum of 2 months' notice from the intended session date. This allows time for sourcing materials and preparing your session properly.
Deposit
A 50% non-refundable deposit is required upon confirmation. This covers materials purchased specifically for your session.
Date Change
One complimentary date change is available per booking. The new date is subject to availability. Please give as much notice as possible.
Cancellation
Deposits are non-refundable. In the event of cancellation, the deposit will not be returned as materials will already have been ordered.
